Our Story

A platform for students who care about child health.

EvoSol Pediatrics spreads awareness about pediatric health and wellbeing while empowering families, students, and communities with knowledge. We support physical, emotional, social, and cognitive health from infancy through adolescence by creating educational resources, programs, and service opportunities.

Our work is rooted in the belief that students can contribute meaningfully to healthcare advocacy when given structure, mentorship, and a community.

Research Program

EvoSol Summer Research Internship

A multi-week immersive program combining expert webinars, guided projects, and final presentations to pediatric health professionals.

About ESRI

The EvoSol Summer Research Internship gives students meaningful, structured exposure to pediatric health research. Participants collaborate with peers, receive guidance, and develop skills in health research, communication, and advocacy.

What participants do
  • Attend expert-led webinars on pediatric health topics
  • Complete guided research projects on real healthcare issues
  • Develop a final report and presentation
  • Present findings to a panel of pediatric health professionals
  • Join a global community of student health advocates
Who can apply

Open to high school and undergraduate students interested in pediatric medicine, healthcare advocacy, public health, or research. No prior research experience is required.

Mentorship Program

EvoSol Mentorship Program

Personalized guidance from mentors to help students grow their research, projects, and career interests in pediatrics.

About EMP

EMP pairs motivated students with experienced professionals or advanced students in pediatric health and related fields. Mentors provide support, feedback, career insights, and guidance on research or advocacy projects.

Program benefits
  • One-on-one mentorship and career exploration
  • Feedback on research ideas and project development
  • Networking and professional communication practice
  • Resources tailored to student goals

Initiative

Emergency Pediatric Lifeline Initiative

Strengthening pediatric emergency response so children can access life-saving care when it matters most.

About EPLI

EPLI is EvoSol Pediatrics' global project focused on pediatric emergency preparedness and response.

  • Education campaigns on pediatric emergency preparedness
